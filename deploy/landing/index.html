Explore a design

Try the included backpack bracket or upload an STL up to 20 MB. Change filament, infill, walls, build direction, and the applied force to see how the preview changes.

Know the limits

This early preview does not predict failure or establish safe working loads. Coarse meshes can understate bending movement. Use it to explore ideas; verify real parts through suitable analysis and testing.

Use non-sensitive parts

The playground processes uploads on an isolated preview server. Parts expire after 15 minutes and clear from memory on the next one-minute cleanup. Each browser has a separate temporary workspace.
